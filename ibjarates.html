About IBJA Rates

IBJA Gold prices are India's Benchmark rates. Authentic source for daily Gold and Silver prices with historical metals rates. Gold rate daily opening and closing rates for India.

India Bullion and Jewellers Association (IBJA) is a 107 year old association based in Zaveri Bazar, Mumbai, India. IBJA publishes daily Gold opening (AM) and closing (PM) Rates. IBJA Gold rates are benchmark rates for issue of Sovereign Gold Bonds as per various notifications issued by the Ministry of Finance and Reserve Bank of India. IBJA Gold and Silver rates are also benchmark rates for Lending against Jewellery by NBFC and Bank as per RBI notifications. IBJA Rates are released for various purity (Gold 999, Gold 995, Gold 916, Gold 750, Gold 585 and Silver 999)
